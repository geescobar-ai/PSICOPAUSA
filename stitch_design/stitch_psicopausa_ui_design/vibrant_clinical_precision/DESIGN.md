## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a vibrant, high-energy, yet clinically precise digital workspace. Tailored for dynamic healthcare environments and fast-paced clinical workflows, the interface balances modern vibrancy with rigorous functional clarity.

The visual direction embraces **High-Impact Modernism**, utilizing bold structural geometry, vivid electric blues, and clean typographic contrast. Whitespace is optimized for speed and scannability, empowering practitioners and trainees to process critical data rapidly without cognitive fatigue.

## Colors

The palette establishes an energetic and professional clinical baseline using vivid blues and crisp neutrals:

- **Primary Brand Tier**:
  - `primary`: `#00a9fd` (Electric Clinical Blue) represents active engagement, technological clarity, and trust.
  - `primary-container`: `#e0f2fe` serves as subtle emphasis and supportive fills.
- **Secondary Professional Tier**:
  - `secondary`: `#1279cf` (Deep Vibrant Blue) offers structural contrast and navigational emphasis.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#f8fafc` provides a clean, modern canvas.
  - `surface-container`: `#e2e8f0` separates toolbars and framing.

## Typography

The typography relies on **Archivo Narrow** for headlines and **Barlow Condensed** for body text and labels, delivering a compact, highly efficient, and modern aesthetic optimized for high-density clinical data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a responsive grid system, maintaining clear column structures and comfortable breathing space across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through clean tonal stratification and subtle shadows that maintain a crisp, modern profile.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced, moderately rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring a friendly yet professional appearance across all components.

## Components

Components are styled for high contrast, immediate legibility, and rapid interactive response, utilizing the vibrant primary and secondary color tokens.
